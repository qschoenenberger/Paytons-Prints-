# Payton's Prints — Marketing Card

4x6 two-sided marketing card for Payton's Prints (custom pen & ink illustration), built as a Claude Design canvas.

Live editable artifact: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/45223a03-684f-404b-96aa-7843fa6f6d09

## What's here

- `design-canvas/` — the editable source: four artboards (`Main.dc.html` / `Back.dc.html` for the standard 6x4in landscape card, `MainVertical.dc.html` / `BackVertical.dc.html` for the 5.5x8.5in half-sheet portrait version), `canvas.json` (layout manifest), and the source images/QR code used in the collage.
- `print-ready-pdfs/` — ready-to-print PDFs at exact print scale:
  - `paytons-prints-front-4x6.pdf` / `paytons-prints-back-4x6.pdf` — standard 6x4in landscape card, front and back.
  - `paytons-prints-front-halfsheet-5.5x8.5.pdf` / `paytons-prints-back-halfsheet-5.5x8.5.pdf` — 5.5x8.5in half-sheet portrait version, front and back (single card).
  - `paytons-prints-vertical-2up-print-sheet.pdf` — 2-page PDF on 11x8.5in landscape letter paper: page 1 has two card fronts side by side, page 2 has two card backs side by side. Print both, cut down the middle, and you get 2 finished double-sided 5.5x8.5in cards from one sheet.

## Card content

- Front: tight-cropped collage of 7 pen-and-ink illustrations.
- Back: "Payton's Prints" wordmark, bio copy, and a scannable QR code to the shop.
